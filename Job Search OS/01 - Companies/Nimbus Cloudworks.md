## Why this company
- Builds a managed Kubernetes platform for mid-market SaaS teams — directly matches my Docker/K8s/Terraform stack.
- Fully remote, async-friendly engineering culture (mentioned in three separate Glassdoor reviews).

- Uses Go for their control plane services and Python for internal tooling.
